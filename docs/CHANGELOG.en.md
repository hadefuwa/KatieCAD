# Changes

## 1.1.3
- New: **Project > "Evaluate single-pole representation too"** (tick, also in the "Create documentation" dialog): when set, single-pole symbols are evaluated like normal ones (lists, numbering, checks, net, PLC), so purely single-pole projects produce lists as well. Without the tick everything stays as before (never counted). The setting is saved per project.
- New: **Make 3D parts larger/smaller with "Stretch"** (same command as in 2D: Edit > Stretch / `ST`): select 3D objects, click the base point on the side to move (e.g. top edge of the cabinet), click the target point or type the distance (e.g. `200` - towards the mouse, `-200` reverses). Large parts (carcass, door, side panels) become longer/shorter, small parts on that side move along, the rest stays. Not stretchable: bodies on work planes, spheres, merged bodies, bodies with cut-outs.
- Fix: **Switching sheets with the 3D view open** failed with `TclError: window ".!canvas" isn't packed`; the 3D view is now closed cleanly / the display is anchored correctly.
- New: **3D objects can be copied to another sheet with Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V:** in the 3D view the copy hangs on the mouse after Ctrl+V (with object snap) and is put down with a click. Fix: pasting in the 2D view placed a 3D body wrongly (the reference point was 0/0)
- New: **3D - exact distance when moving:** while dragging an axis arrow just type the number (e.g. `300`, Backspace deletes, `-` = opposite direction) and confirm with Enter; also after clicking the arrow or via the input line
- New: **3D - object snap when moving and placing also with the edge midpoints of the moving body** (previously only its corners); the edge midpoints of existing bodies were already snapped
- Fix: **moved mask texts (IND, MAN, TYP, PER, number of poles) keep their position on "Create device"** (they used to jump back to the default position); moving a text later on the placed device overrides the preset
- New: **F1 opens an overview of all keyboard shortcuts, mouse actions and commands** (scrollable, with search field; also Help > Keyboard shortcuts and commands). The command list is generated from the program's command table, so it stays complete
- New: generator `tools/make_symbols_einpolig.py` for **single-pole symbols** (folder "12 Single-pole", attribute POLE for the pole count, attribute STRICH for the pole stroke "/" (empty = none), tick "Single-pole representation" preset); writes only into its own output folder
- New: **number of poles for your own single-pole devices** (field "Number of poles" in the device dialog, e.g. 3 or 3+N, shown exactly as typed above the identifier)
- New: **single-pole representation as second drawing:** tick "Single-pole representation" in the dialog of device mask, block, terminal and cable. Symbols marked this way are counted in no list (device, parts, terminal, cable, PLC list), not in the net, not in cross references, not in the project check and not in auto-numbering - drawing the same device single- and multi-pole produces no duplicates
- New: **walls, doors and windows for building floor plans** (Insert > Wall / Door in wall / Window in wall): adjustable wall thickness and position of the line, own layer "Wände", mitred corners, object snap on all wall edges; doors and windows are objects of their own (placed into the wall by click, movable and resizable by grips, double click = only their size, hinge side and opening direction), a door cuts the gap into the wall automatically
- New: **join walls** (click two walls > Insert > Join walls): both become one wall with a mitred corner, centre lines extended or trimmed to their intersection
- New: **hatch walls** directly in the wall dialog (double click) or via right click > Hatch wall: diagonal or cross, adjustable line spacing, door openings stay free
- New: **"Join walls" button** in the toolbar: click the first and second wall, they are merged at once into one wall with a mitred corner
- New: wall hatch also as **solid fill**; default line spacing 180 mm
- Fix: hatch ran through doors and windows; doors and windows on the wall line (position "left/right") were sometimes not recognised in the wall and cut no gap
- Change: **the separate "Change dimension text" window is gone:** double click on a dimension opens its properties again (with the field "Displayed text", the measured value and "Restore measured value", arrow size, end piece ...); the text is also in the properties window (Ctrl+1)
- Fix: **properties window for dimensions:** text height, font and arrow size can be set again (the fields were locked for dimensions)
- New: **end piece of dimensions selectable:** arrow or **diagonal tick** (properties window, properties dialog and toolbar); font of the dimension text adjustable
- New: **decimal places of dimensions** adjustable (0 to 6): in the properties window (Ctrl+1, also for several dimensions at once) and via right click on the dimension > Decimal places
- New: **door models:** single leaf, double door (two equal leaves), double door with one larger leaf (share adjustable, side switchable) and roller shutter (dashed in the wall opening). Choosing "Door" / "Window" first opens the properties window (model, size, hinge), the values apply to all following clicks; double click on a placed door changes it afterwards
- New: **Rooms** (Insert > Room, "Room" button, command `ROOM`): click the corner points of the room (any number, also L-shaped rooms; a click on the first point closes it), enter room number and room name - the area in m² is calculated automatically. The box stays invisible, only the text (number, name, area) is shown; clicking the text shows the box with corner grips to change the room size at will (the area is recalculated); the centre grip moves the text only, double click opens number/name. Font (also bold) and text height can be set in the room dialog. Own layer "Räume", output as text in PDF and DXF
- New: **Close wall:** clicking the same wall twice in the join tool (or right click > Close wall) joins start and end with a clean mitred corner
- Fix: **doors and windows cut their gap and hatch correctly only in the first segment of a wall** (in all further segments, e.g. rooms made of one wall, the hatch ran through the door)
- New: **walls form one common outline automatically:** where walls touch, cross or overlap, faces, end caps and hatch inside the other wall are no longer drawn (T junctions, crossings, overhangs)
- New: **T junction with "Join walls":** if the end of a wall is in front of or in the middle of another, it is extended or trimmed onto its centre line
- Fix: **Ortho** now also applies when dragging wall and polyline points by their grips (relative to the neighbouring point)
- Fix: **"Join walls"** got stuck after the first click (the click was pulled onto the object snap and hit the first wall again); now the actual click position counts, the second wall must be a different one, afterwards the tool returns to selection
- Fix: if several walls lie on top of each other, doors and windows now cut into all of them (before, the hatch of the other wall stayed)
- Change: the layer "Wände" has the colour #c29bff by default
- Change: program version 1.1.3 (About window, installer)
- New: **Hide cabinet doors per viewport** (right-click the viewport > 3D view in this viewport > Hide cabinet doors): door, handle and lock disappear in this viewport only; the same cabinet thus appears in one layout with and without door, without drawing it twice. Saved with the project, applies to object snap and PDF

- New: **terminals (and device masks) with a "show" check box per field** (IND, MAN, TYP, PER, NUM): hidden fields stay available for lists and evaluation but do not appear in the drawing; the others move up without a gap
- Fix: **export project as DXF** failed with "No page could be created" (since the fonts of 1.1.1 the DXF page received font arguments it did not know); the message now also names the actual reason
- New: **Windows installer with the KatieCAD banner**: welcome and finish page show mascot and logo, the other pages the mascot at the top right (several sizes for high screen resolutions), own welcome text, publisher and support links; images made from `installer/KatieCAD_Setup_Banner.png` by `tools/make_installer_images.py`
- New: **Linux AppImage**: the program as a single, directly runnable file (`KatieCAD-<version>-Linux-x86_64.AppImage`), no folder and no installation; built by the release workflow together with .deb and .tar.gz (`installer/build_appimage.sh`)
- Change: **minimum size of the main window** (1010 × 600 pixels, down to the screen size on small screens); the window can no longer be made so small that the program becomes unusable
- Change: **viewport view snaps while turning** (middle mouse button, view cube) onto Front/Back/Left/Right/Top/Bottom when within 4 degrees, so dimensions in the layout show true lengths
- Fix: **right-click in the layout over a viewport** only showed the viewport menu, even for dimensions/texts on it (no move/copy with base point); now the menu of the object under the pointer opens, the viewport menu additionally has move/copy
- Fix: a **cabinet without door and without mounting plate** was a closed box with "Hide hidden edges"; it is now open at the front (front taken from the plan, also rotated/mirrored)
- New: **change dimension text**: double-click a dimension or right-click > "Change dimension text..." (command `MASSTEXT`) opens its own dialog for any text (e.g. 150), `<>` = measured value, with a "Restore measured value" button
- New: **object snap in the layout on viewport content** (2D: all snap kinds, 3D: corners, edge midpoints, centres, without activating the viewport); **dimensions in the layout** inside a viewport show the **model value** instead of paper mm
- New: **move/copy from point to point in 3D**: click base point (e.g. a corner) and target point, both snap to corners, edge midpoints and centres, with dashed preview; right-click menu with "Move/Copy with base point"; commands V/M/CO and the Edit menu now work in space in the 3D window; new button in the 3D bar
- New: **view cube and "Hidden" button in the layout**: a selected/active 3D viewport shows the view cube at the top right (face click = standard view, arrows, free rotation) and a button to hide hidden edges; on screen only, not in print/PDF
- Change: **save questions** for unsaved changes (open, quit, open/create/close project) now name the project and sheet in a project and state that "No" discards the changes
- New: **message boxes in the design of the program** (colour scheme, Yes/No/Cancel, Y/N keys, Enter/Esc) instead of the system boxes
- Change: **File > New** always asks: with unsaved changes "Save changes?" (Yes/No/Cancel), otherwise a clear confirmation (default: No); in a project File > New closes the **whole project** (after asking "Save the changes in the project?"); sheets are managed in the side bar only
- New: **own menu bar now on Windows as well**: looks the same everywhere and follows the colour scheme (the Windows menu bar was always white); macOS keeps its native menu bar; set the environment variable `KATIECAD_NATIVE_MENU=1` to force the native bar
- Fix: the About window showed a thin line left of the mascot (1 pixel edge in `KatieCAD_Maskottchen.png`); removed
- Fix (Linux): **sub-menus of the menu bar** (View > Colour scheme, Place toolbars, also nested) could not be opened; they now open by mouse (hover/click) and keyboard (arrows, Enter, Esc); the menu bar follows the colour scheme
- New: in the **light colour scheme** the About window shows the lettering with a dark "Katie" (file `KatieCAD_Banner_dunkel.png`); violet and black keep the light lettering
- Change: **drawing background (model and layout) is a program setting** and no longer stored in the drawing/project file; foreign files open with your own background, old files stay readable
- New: **colour scheme** (View > Colour scheme): Violet (default), Black and Light for menus, bars, side bars and dialogs; applied at once and remembered
- New: **merge 3D bodies** (menu 3D, `3DVERSCHMELZEN`, right-click): several bodies become one body by boolean union, inner edges and overlaps disappear, holes are kept, saved with the project, can be merged again with further bodies
- New: **Manufacturer field `MAN`** in the device mask (order IND, MAN, TYP, PER, REF); *Manufacturer* column in the device list; the parts list uses MAN (otherwise the parts catalogue) and keeps the same type from different manufacturers apart. All symbols get MAN via `tools/symbole_man_ergaenzen.py`
- New: **Hide hidden edges** in the 3D view (button in the 3D bar): bodies look solid, edges behind them disappear, cabinets read unambiguously; a cabinet without a door is open at the front (mounting plate visible)
- New: **Hidden edges in the layout and PDF**: setting per viewport (right-click > 3D view), stored with the project and written to the PDF
- New: **terminals and cables** have the field `MAN` as well (terminal plan, cable plan, device list, parts list)
- Change: readable column titles in the lists: *ID* instead of IND, *Type designation* instead of TYP, *Rating* instead of PER (Terminal strip (ID)); the drawings keep the short tags
- Version jump to 1.1.2
